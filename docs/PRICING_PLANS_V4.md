### What they lock behind the top plan (our opening)
- **MagicSlides**: AI edits, the advanced editor, 200+ templates, PNG/JPG/HTML export, premium AI models and share analytics are **Premium only** (₹833 / $23.25). Pro gets only 15 slides per deck.

## 2. What we have (built and proven)

## 4. Margins
Net revenue is after 18% GST (India), Razorpay about 2% + GST (India), and card fees about 3% + $0.30 (international), at ₹88 per $. The cost is the **measured** $0.745 per 1,000 credits (`PRICING_BURN_RESULTS.md`, a deliberately heavy mix with many AI pictures). "Worst" means the customer spends every credit; "typical" means a quarter, which is normal for subscriptions.

| Plan | India yearly: net → worst / typical margin | India monthly: worst | International yearly: worst / typical | International monthly: worst |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | costs us at most **$0.07** per user per month | | | |
| Lite | $2.34 → **68% / 92%** | 74% | $4.55 → **84% / 96%** | 89% |
| Pro | $3.75 → **40% / 85%** | 52% | $9.40 → **76% / 94%** | 83% |
| Gold | $7.51 → **41% / 85%** | 60% | $18.13 → **75% / 94%** | 81% |

For comparison, **today's Gold in India billed yearly (₹960, 10,000 credits) is only 17% in the worst case**: the cut to 6,000 credits is what fixes it. India yearly Pro and Gold (≈40% worst case) are the tightest rows, and they only reach that if a customer spends every credit on the costly mix.

Guards that keep the margins safe (already built): monthly picture caps, model tiers per plan, the daily AI spending cap per account ($6), one account per mailbox with e-mail confirmation, and credits that never roll over.

## 5. Rules to keep
- Prices, credits and features are database data (`plan`, `plan_price`, `credit_cost`); the pricing page text is generated from them.
- Yearly saves about 20% (Pro: ₹399 vs ₹499), shown as "2 months free"-style messaging.
- Suggested refund policy, in line with competitors: full refund within **7 days of the first payment** if under 20% of the credits were used; renewals are not refunded; cancel any time and keep the plan to the end of the period.

## 7. Model quality: Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite (2 credits/slide) vs Gemini 3.6 Flash (5 credits/slide) (2026-10-30)
Same 6 prompts (party plan, board security review, poetry lecture, fintech pitch, sales review with given numbers, Hindi health talk), 10 slides each, real AI, one run each (`e2e/compare-models.mjs`, report: `services/api/scripts/model-compare-report.ts`).

| Measured (average per deck) | Flash Lite | 3.6 Flash |
|---|---|---|
| Words per slide | 55 | **79** |
| Words per bullet | 6.4 | **9.0** |
| Words per slide title | 3.6 | **5.4** |
| Generic titles ("Agenda", "Churn Analysis") per deck | 1.0 | 0.3 |
| Different layouts used | 7.0 | **7.8** (Hindi deck: Lite used only 4) |
| Numbers the user never gave | **0.8** (5 invented budget figures in the party deck) | 0 |
| Overflowing or too-small text | 0 | 0 |
| Second-model review pass | none (starter tier is skipped) | yes |
| Real cost per deck | $0.01 | $0.02 |

Reading the text side by side: 3.6 Flash writes fuller, more specific sentences and descriptive titles; Flash Lite is terse and generic ("Budget planning in rupees for all items"). But 3.6 Flash **embellishes**: in the sales deck it wrote "Outbound sales initiatives drove a significant portion of new accounts", which the user never said. Both write correct Hindi.
**Conclusion:** the real cost gap is about **1 cent per deck**, so Lite saves us almost nothing while giving noticeably weaker decks; the 2-versus-5 credit gap is a price choice, not a cost difference. Suggested: make Gemini 3.6/3.8 Flash the default for every plan including Free and lower its price to about 3 credits per slide (text-only cost then ≈ $0.00045 per credit against about $0.00125 net revenue per credit on India yearly Pro: ≈ 64% margin); keep Flash Lite as an optional "fast" choice only. Re-run `pricing:model` before changing `credit_cost`. Limits of this test: 6 prompts, one run each, judged by reading plus counts; timings are not comparable (12 runs in parallel on the development provider).
