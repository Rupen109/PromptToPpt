# SEO / AEO keyword plan (draft, 2026-10-28)

**How this was made, and its limits.** No paid SEO tool and no search-volume numbers were available. The queries below are *real Google autocomplete suggestions* (1,106 unique queries harvested from 20 seed phrases × modifiers; full list in `docs/seo/keywords-autocomplete-2026-10-28.csv`). A query that many different seeds surface is a stronger signal than one that appears once, but it is **not** a volume. Competition was judged by looking at who appears for each phrase (web search), not by a difficulty score. **Next step: load the CSV into Google Keyword Planner (free with an Ads account) or Ubersuggest and add real volumes and difficulty before committing writing time.**

Brand name under test: **PromptSlides** (promptslides.app). Trademark, India search, Instagram/Facebook/TikTok handles and the "ask ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity what it is" test are still the owner's to do.

## Product facts that decide which keywords are honest to target
- Free plan (Starter) has a **watermark and no export**, and generation needs a **login**. So "…without watermark" and "…without login" (very frequent in autocomplete) are **not** keywords we can satisfy today. Do not build pages for them until the product supports it (or accept high bounce).
- Strong real features: topic/prompt → deck, PDF / Word / Excel / email / link sources, Wikipedia + web facts with citations, 120 languages (incl. Hindi), brand kits, free PDF→PPT tool, templates, editable PPTX export (Pro/Gold).

## AEO (being named/cited by AI answers)
Question-style queries seen in autocomplete: *what is ai slides · can chatgpt make ppt · how to make ppt using ai · how to convert pdf to ppt · how can i make ppt on mobile*. Answer each in a short, factual page with a direct first-sentence answer, steps, a comparison table, and FAQ schema. AI assistants mostly repeat what many independent sites say about a brand: Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, TopAI/"There's An AI For That", G2/Capterra, Reddit, YouTube tutorials, listicles. Keep the name, one-line description and logo identical everywhere ("PromptSlides — AI presentation maker: turn a prompt, PDF or notes into an editable PPT").

## Brand-name hygiene
Always write **PromptSlides** as one word. Add Organization + SoftwareApplication structured data, `sameAs` links to every profile, Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools. "Prompt slides" alone is read by Google as "slides about prompts", so keep "AI presentation maker" next to the name in titles.

## Realistic timeline (no guarantees)
Weeks: brand-name search once indexed · 3–6 months: first long-tail traffic · 6–12 months: page-1 chances for Tier A · 12+ months: any head term.
